Your final grade is built from four components. Pay close attention to the hurdle requirement in the final exam.

3.1 In‑class Quizzes (10 %)

  • There are 10 quizzes across Weeks 1-10 (submitted via Blackboard before the end of each lecture).
  • Each quiz is worth 1.66 marks.
  • Your final score is calculated from your best 6 out of 10 quizzes.
  • Because only the best 6 of 10 contribute and answers are released soon after, no late submissions or extensions are permitted without a valid central extension request.

3.2 Data Analysis Project (20 %)

You will apply the statistical techniques taught in class to a provided engineering dataset to estimate a statistical model and explain the data generating process.

The report must be fully reproducible. You must submit both: 1. The .qmd (or .rmd) source file. 2. The rendered report (PDF, HTML, or MS Word format).

Submit via Turnitin on Blackboard by 6 October 2026, 4:00 pm.

3.3 Problem Set (20 %)

A set of applied problems covering statistics, data processing, and numerical optimisation. This assessment is designed to help you prepare for the final exam.

Submit via Turnitin on Blackboard by 28 October 2026, 4:00 pm.

3.4 Final Exam (50 %): Hurdle Requirement

  • In‑person, identity‑verified examination during the official exam period (7 November 2026-21 November 2026).
  • Hurdle requirement: You must achieve at least 40 % on the final exam to pass the course with a Grade 4 or higher.
  • Format: Closed book paper-based exam (10 mins planning, 120 mins duration). Permitted materials: One double-sided A4 sheet of handwritten notes and a UQ approved calculator (Casio FX82 series). Generative AI / MT tools are strictly prohibited.
  • Most students who fail ENGG7518 do so because they do not reach this 40 % hurdle.
ImportantFinal exam Q&A

To ensure fairness, I will not answer individual questions about the final exam. Instead, the last tutorial of the semester will be entirely dedicated to exam preparation. Bring all your questions there.

3.5 Late Submission & Extensions

  • All assessments must be submitted on or before the due date.

  • If you need an extension (medical, family emergency, etc.), you must apply before the due date via the central UQ system:
    https://my.uq.edu.au/information-and-services/manage-my-program/exams-and-assessment/applying-extension

  • Do not email me directly for extensions; they are centrally managed.

Late penalty (without approved extension):

  • 10 % of the maximum possible mark deducted per 24‑hour period (including weekends).
  • After 7 calendar days, the mark becomes 0.
  • Example: For an assignment worth 20 marks, a 2‑day late submission loses 4 marks.

All written assignments must be submitted through Turnitin on Blackboard.
