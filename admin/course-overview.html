2.2 Course Description

This postgraduate course equips you with the fundamental tools to design, execute, and communicate engineering research. It is organised into four balanced blocks:

  • Fundamental concepts of research design (experimental & observational): ~25%
  • Statistical methods (modelling, handling empirical data, interpretation): ~25%
  • Data processing and analysis techniques: ~25%
  • Numerical optimisation: ~25%

We will use real engineering datasets in class. You will gain hands‑on experience with the statistical software R (and optionally Excel) during tutorials and assignments.

2.3 Course Aims

After completing ENGG7518 you should be able to:

  1. Understand research design in experimental and non‑experimental engineering settings.
  2. Estimate statistical models, interpret output, and communicate findings.
  3. Use common mathematical and computational methods to analyse data and extract useful information.
  4. Recognise and solve simple optimisation problems that arise in engineering contexts.

2.4 Learning Outcomes

After successfully completing this course you should be able to:

  1. LO1: Explain the basic elements and process of conducting academic research, including but not limited to framing research questions, developing hypotheses, conducting critical literature reviews, designing experiments or observational studies, ethics issues and fallacies & obstacles to scientific research.
  2. LO2: Identify and cope with common data issues, and carry out descriptive data analysis.
  3. LO3: Estimate basic statistical models, interpret their findings, and convey results in a meaningful manner.
  4. LO4: Explain the basic principles of numerical optimisation problems in Engineering, how they arise, and how to solve basic problems.
  5. LO5: Explain basic concepts, theories, and procedures of some commonly used optimisation methods in Engineering.
  6. LO6: Articulate the limitations of your research and how to remain objective when reviewing your own research or research of others.
  7. LO7: Write and format your research report professionally and communicate research results in a reproducible manner.
